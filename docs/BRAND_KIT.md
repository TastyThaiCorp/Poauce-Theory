## 1. Name Lock-In Check

### What the search found (2026-10-05)

**Red flags, not dealbreakers. Read before you print anything.**

- **LIVE pending USPTO application: "SAUCED"**, owner Pur Vin Holdings Co., Nashville, TN. Serial 98853827 and 98853819, filed 2026-11-14. One filing is Class 33 (Wine & Spirits); the filings tie to "Sauced Wine Bar." Status: live, pending registration. If it registers, they get nationwide presumption of rights in their classes.
- **Sauced Potstickers** (saucedpotstickers.com): operating potsticker restaurant brand. Common-law food use of the same name.
- **"Sauced: 30 Totally Bitchin' Bowls"**: cookbook published 2026-01 by the Bitchin' Sauce founder. Food-adjacent use of the word as a brand.
- **Sauce (getsauce.com)**: commission-free restaurant ordering platform, an Instagram ordering partner. Adjacent food-tech name.
- **SAUCE BAE**: live USPTO mark (serial 98205214) covering retail/wholesale of condiments including hot sauce. Close neighbor, not identical.

### What this means

- "Sauced" is crowded in food and beverage. A pending federal application plus operating restaurants using the name = real common-law risk (cease-and-desist letters, opposition if you file).
- "Sauced" also risks a **merely descriptive** refusal at the USPTO for sauce products: it literally describes what the product is.
- None of this kills the name. It means: do a proper clearance search before spending on packaging, and decide your risk tolerance.

### USPTO / trademark note

- Proper clearance = search the USPTO Trademark Search system (tmsearch.uspto.gov), plus state registries and common-law web/social use. A trademark attorney clearance search is the thorough option; DIY TESS search is the fast option.
- Federal filing via TEAS Plus runs **~$250-350 per class (estimate)**. Likely classes: 30 (staple foods, sauces, flavorings) and 35 (online retail/wholesale store services). Possibly 29 depending on final product classification.
- **Filing, and whether to use an attorney, is Jor's decision.** Do not order packaging with the name on it until you decide.

## 4. Brand Voice

Register: direct, punchy, numbers-forward. Playful premium, never silly.

### Pillar 1: Punchy, not precious
- Do: "8 flavors. 4 categories. Zero boring bites."
- Don't: "Embark on a sensorial journey of flavor."

### Pillar 2: Numbers-forward
- Do: "8 oz jar. 8 launch flavors. 100% plant-based beads."
- Don't: "Our amazing, incredible, world-class flavor experience."

### Pillar 3: Playful, never childish
- Do: "Pop one. Then try to stop."
- Don't: "Yummy nummy beads for your tummy!"

### Pillar 4: Confident, never preachy
- Do: "Agar, not gelatin. Halal by default."
- Don't: "Unlike other brands, we care about your dietary choices and the planet."

**House rules:** no em dashes in customer-facing copy. Short sentences. Verbs over adjectives.

## 6. Packaging Direction: 8 oz PET Jar Label

**Format:** clear PET jar, 8 oz (227 g), full-wrap or front/back label on Rice Milk `#FBF6EC` base.

**Front-of-pack hierarchy (top to bottom):**

1. **SAUCED wordmark** (logo lockup, top, Chili Lacquer on Rice Milk)
2. **Flavor name** (Display font, all caps, large: e.g. BROWN SUGAR)
3. **Category band** (color-coded strip: Mango Glow / Sriracha Ember / Sea Pickle Teal / Miso Depth, with category word: SWEET, SAVORY, SALTY, UMAMI)
4. **Descriptor line:** "Spherified flavor beads"
5. **Badge row (small icons):** Plant-based | Halal | Gelatin-free | Pork-free
6. **Net weight:** 8 oz (227 g), bottom right

**Design notes:**

- Category band wraps the bottom third of the label so flavors are scannable on a shelf or in a TikTok Shop grid.
- One glossy bead illustration per flavor, tinted to the actual bead color, floating near the flavor name.
- Back panel: ingredients, nutrition facts, "Best on:" serving ideas (3 per flavor), QR code to TikTok (@sauced or fallback handle).
- No pastel gradients. Flat color, bold type, high contrast. The jar shows the real beads, so the label stays graphic and clean.
