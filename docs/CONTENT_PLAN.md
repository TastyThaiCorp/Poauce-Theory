## 7. Metrics to Track

Track weekly in a simple sheet. Columns: post date, platform, pillar, views, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, link clicks, jars sold.

**Metrics that matter, in order:**

1. **Views:** Reach. Are the hooks stopping the scroll?
2. **Saves:** Intent. Saved = "I want to try this later." Strongest buying signal on TikTok.
3. **Shares:** Virality. Shared = "Someone else needs to see this."
4. **Profile visits:** Curiosity. Are viewers checking who is behind this?
5. **Link clicks:** Purchase intent. Clicks to the store page.
6. **Conversion:** Clicks that become $11.99 jar orders.

**Targets by Day 30 (targets, not promises):**

- Total TikTok views across 30 posts: 150,000+
- Average saves per post: 200+ (saves are the buy signal for food products)
- At least 2 posts over 25,000 views (expect the oil-drop macro and one savory shock post to lead)
- Profile visits: 5,000+ cumulative
- Link clicks: 1,500+ cumulative (roughly 1% of total views)
- Conversion: 2-3% of link clicks to purchase = 30-45 jars sold DTC in month one
- Follower growth: +2,000 on TikTok, +500 on Instagram
- Reels: 4-5 posts/week, target 30,000+ cumulative views

**Read the numbers weekly and adjust:**

- Saves high but clicks low: the product is interesting but the offer is weak. Push price and link harder (Hook 3 rotation).
- Views high but saves low: entertainment without intent. Add more use-case demos (ramen, burger, cocktail).
- Comments asking "is it halal" or "what is agar": pin an FAQ comment, then make it a full video (Day 21 covers this).
- One flavor outselling the rest: double down with dedicated content for that flavor in month two.
