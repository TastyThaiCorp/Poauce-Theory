## 2. Recommendation: Shopify Basic (annual billing)

**Recommended platform:** Shopify Basic at $29/mo annual billing.

**Why:**
- Best single system for all three revenue motions: DTC one-time, subscriptions (repeat snack/condiment purchases), and wholesale B2B price lists.
- TikTok Shop integration matters: Jor is a TikTok creator, and shoppable video is the natural channel for a visually fun product like popping sauced beads.
- App ecosystem is the deepest for food brands: nutrition label apps, subscription billing, review apps, cold-chain shipping rules.
- The separate-company requirement is trivial: a brand-new Shopify account and store, cleanly isolated from Tasty Thai's Square setup.

**First-year cost math (estimate, ~200 orders/mo, $12 average order):**
- Monthly platform fee: $29 x 12 = $348
- Transaction fees: (12 x 0.029 + 0.30) = $0.648 per order x 200 = $129.60/mo x 12 = $1,555.20
- **Total first-year platform cost (estimate): $1,903.20**
- Effective platform cost as share of revenue: $1,903.20 / ($2,400/mo x 12) = ~6.6% (estimate)

Note: most orders will likely exceed $12 AOV once 3-packs ($29.99) and variety packs ($79.99) are in the mix, which improves the fee ratio. Single-jar orders are the smallest-fee-efficiency case shown above.

**Runner-up: Square Online (free plan).** Pick Square Online free if the priority is $0 upfront and same-day launch using tools Jor already knows, with invoicing for wholesale accounts and a POS app for farmers markets and pop-ups. First-year cost at the same 200 orders/mo (estimate): $0 + (12 x 0.033 + 0.30) = $0.696 x 200 = $139.20/mo x 12 = **$1,670.40**.

**When to switch to Shopify:** when any one of these becomes true: (1) subscriptions are ready to launch, (2) wholesale ordering needs a self-serve portal rather than manual invoices, or (3) TikTok Shop or paid social becomes a serious channel. Switching cost is low while order volume is small, so starting on Square free and migrating later is a legitimate two-stage path. Do not start on Wix: cheapest monthly fee, but the weakest wholesale and subscription story, which are core to the Sauced model.

## 6. FAQ Draft

(No em dashes used in customer-facing copy.)

1. **What are Sauced beads?**
   Small popping beads made with real sauces and flavors, set with agar (a seaweed-based gelling agent). Each bead bursts with flavor. Think boba texture, but the flavor is the sauce itself: brown sugar, sriracha, miso, pickle brine, and more.

2. **Are they halal?**
   Yes. Sauced beads are halal. They are also gelatin-free and pork-free. The gelling agent is agar, which is plant-based.

3. **Do they contain gelatin?**
   No. All Sauced beads are gelatin-free. We use agar instead of gelatin in every flavor.

4. **What is the shelf life?**
   Unopened jars: check the best-by date printed on each jar (estimate: several months refrigerated, exact dating confirmed at production). After opening: refrigerate and use within the timeframe printed on the label (estimate: 2-3 weeks; final guidance confirmed at production).

5. **How does shipping work? Are they perishable?**
   Yes, Sauced beads are a refrigerated product. Orders ship with cold packs in insulated packaging. Cold-pack shipping adds roughly $8-14 per order (estimate) on top of standard shipping, which is reflected in our shipping rates at checkout. Local Denver delivery is available as a lower-cost option.

6. **How do I use the savory beads?**
   Like a finishing sauce with texture. Spoon Spicy Sriracha beads over ramen, fried rice, or wings. Garlic Soy beads go on sushi rice, dumplings, or poke. White Miso and Shiitake Soy beads finish soups, noodles, and roasted vegetables. About 50g (one serving) per dish.

7. **How do I use the sweet beads?**
   Like boba, but better. Brown Sugar beads go in milk tea, over ice cream, or on pancakes. Mango Passionfruit beads top yogurt, sparkling water, shaved ice, and cocktails or mocktails.

8. **What are the wholesale minimums?**
   Wholesale is sold in cases of 12 jars at $6.00 per jar ($72 per case). Minimum opening order: 2 cases (24 jars, $144). Reorders: 1 case minimum. Apply at /wholesale/apply.

9. **What allergens are in the beads?**
   Allergen information is listed on every product page and jar label. Flavors containing soy (Garlic Soy, White Miso, Shiitake Soy) contain soy. Some flavors may contain sesame, wheat, or milk depending on the sauce base; check the specific flavor page before buying. Produced in a facility that handles common allergens.

10. **Where are you located? Can I buy locally?**
    Sauced is made in Denver, Colorado. Local pickup and Denver-area delivery are available at launch. Wholesale accounts in the Denver metro get local delivery.

## 7. Launch Pricing and Pack Sizes

| SKU | Price | Jars | Price per jar | Per-unit economics reminder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single jar (any flavor) | $11.99 | 1 | $11.99 | ~75% gross margin DTC (estimate); ~4.5 servings at 50g, ~$2.67/serving (estimate) |
| 3-Pack (pick any 3) | $29.99 | 3 | ~$10.00 | Customer saves $6 vs 3 singles; still ~75% gross margin band (estimate) |
| 8-Flavor Variety Pack | $79.99 | 8 | ~$10.00 | Customer saves ~$16 vs 8 singles; best discovery SKU, giftable |
| Wholesale case | $72.00 | 12 | $6.00 | ~55-58% gross margin (estimate); minimum opening order 2 cases ($144) |

Pack: 8 oz (227g) PET jar, about 4.5 servings at 50g.

**Margin notes (estimates):** DTC single jar at $11.99 with ~75% gross margin implies ~$3.00 cost of goods per jar (estimate). Wholesale at $6.00 with ~55-58% margin implies ~$2.52-2.70 cost of goods per jar (estimate). Cold-pack shipping ($8-14/order estimate) is a fulfillment cost, not COGS; build it into shipping rates or a free-shipping threshold (e.g., free shipping over $50) so it does not eat the product margin.
