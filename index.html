Precision Drip Array

Seventy-two tips, twelve per flavor, each dripping 0.5–2 drops per second from 8–10 cm above the oil.

03

Cold Oil Bath

Twelve liters of grapeseed oil held at 2–5°C, so every bead sets before it lands.

04

Precision Gate Tray

Thirty-six precision gates in brushed steel. HOLD gathers the batch, DRAIN drops the oil, RINSE washes the beads.

05

Oil Reclaim Loop

Used oil is filtered to 50 microns, separated, re-chilled, and returned. Nothing wasted.
