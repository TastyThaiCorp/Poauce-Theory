THE FABRICATOR

Five layers. One pause.

A concept machine for cold-oil spherification at flavor-lab scale.

01

Sauce Reservoir Deck

Six jacketed 1-liter chambers, one per flavor, held at 50–60°C with slow stirrers so the agar never pre-sets.

02

Precision Drip Array

Seventy-two tips, twelve per flavor, each dripping 0.5–2 drops per second from 8–10 cm above the oil.

03

Cold Oil Bath

Twelve liters of grapeseed oil held at 2–5°C in a tall column, so every bead sets before it lands.

04

Iris Aperture Plate

Thirty-six camera-aperture diaphragms. HOLD gathers the batch, DRAIN drops the oil, RINSE washes the beads.

05

Oil Reclaim Loop

Used oil is filtered to 50 microns, separated, re-chilled, and returned. Nothing wasted.

Concept engineering. The iris mechanism and seals need a physical prototype before production.
